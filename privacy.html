Privacy and cookies

Last updated 18 October 2026

Ezzcoins is a fan-made trading guide for EA SPORTS FC 27 Ultimate Team. You can use it without an account. This page explains what is stored when you sign in, what your browser keeps, and how ads work.

Signing in with Google

Sign-in is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, Firebase keeps an account record with your Google name, email address, profile picture and a random account ID, so you can sign in again on any device. Ezzcoins uses this only to sign you in and to show your first name to you. It is never shown to other visitors, sold or used for marketing.

Your watchlist

Your watchlist is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your random account ID. It holds only the player numbers you picked and the time of your last change, with no name or email. The site's price updater reads watchlists to decide which player prices to fetch, so watchlists are technically readable without signing in. They can't be linked to you without access to the Firebase account records.

You can delete your watchlist at any time with Delete my data on the Players tab.

What your browser keeps

  • The last tab you opened, so the site opens there next time.
  • If you sign in, Firebase keeps you signed in using your browser's storage until you sign out.
  • Cached pages, flags, artwork, fonts and icons, so previously visited content can open faster or without a connection. Live prices and uncached content still need an internet connection.

Your builder and trading data

The builders save your squad, recorded purchases and sales, target prices, favourite cards and untradeable-card preferences in this browser. These records are entered by you and are not connected to your EA account. Export lets you keep a copy.

If you sign in with Google and choose Save across devices, your dashboard (trades, targets and saved squad) is stored in its own Firebase document that only your signed-in account can read or change. It is kept apart from your watchlist, so the site’s price updater and other visitors never see it. Loading account data replaces the device dashboard with that saved copy. Signing out switches back to this device’s separate dashboard.

Price targets check while the page is open. Browser notification permission is optional; background push delivery is not active.

Advertising

Ezzcoins shows ads from Google AdSense on phones, tablets and computers. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's My Ad Center, and read how Google uses data from sites like this one in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. If you visit from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown.

Other services

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it sends them.
  • Prices and data come from FUTBIN, FUT.GG, FUTWIZ, EasySBC and EA and are collected by the site’s automated updaters. Your browser loads the saved data from this site and GitHub’s raw-content service. Card artwork can load directly from FUT.GG’s asset service, which receives the connection information needed to deliver the image. Opening a source link takes you to that provider’s website.
  • Flag images and the signature font are stored on this site, so loading them doesn't contact anyone else.

Ezzcoins is a free, fan-made site, not endorsed by or affiliated with EA or its licensors. It is also not affiliated with FUTBIN, FUT.GG, FUTWIZ or EasySBC, and it never sells coins, accounts or boosting.
