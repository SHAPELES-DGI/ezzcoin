About Ezzcoins

Ezzcoins is a free, independent trading desk for EA SPORTS FC 27 Ultimate Team. It watches the Transfer Market around the clock, explains what the numbers mean, and grades its own trade calls in public, losses included.

What you will find here

  • Live console and PC prices for about 20,000 tradeable cards, checked every few minutes, and price graphs for about 3,000 popular cards.
  • A player database with every FC 27 player, filters, and Ezzcoins' own meta rating for each position a card can play.
  • SBC and Evolution builders, a fodder board, a flip calculator that counts the 5% tax, and a log for your own trades.
  • A market read on the home page: the weekly cycle, the 6 PM content drop, Rivals rewards, and the day's biggest risers and fallers.
  • Trading guides and a weekly market recap, written from Ezzcoins' own price records.
  • An automatic trade desk whose every call is graded in public.

Where the numbers come from

Prices are Buy Now quotes read from FUT.GG, and from FUTBIN when it can be reached. Every price on the site says which source it came from and when it was read, and the site checks for new prices every minute. Player ratings and stats are EA SPORTS FC 27's own.

Ezzcoins also keeps an hourly snapshot of the whole market. That record is what the trading guides, the weekly recap and the trade desk's tests are built on: when a guide says that 77% of cards that jumped 15% in a day were cheaper two days later, it is counting Ezzcoins' own snapshots (see Dips and spikes).

How the trade desk works

The desk makes at most two calls a day, each following one of a few written strategies, such as buying fodder after the Thursday Rivals rewards or selling a card after a sharp one-day jump. Every call records the price and a check time when it is made. At the check time it is graded automatically, with EA's 5% tax counted on every buy. A strategy that starts losing is paused, and every 10 minutes the desk replays its strategies on recent prices and switches off any whose paper calls lose, before they cost anyone coins. The full rules and the record are in How calls are graded.

Calls are information, not advice. Prices move fast; check the in-game market before you buy, and only trade coins you can afford to lose.

What Ezzcoins will never do

  • Sell coins, accounts or boosting, or link to anyone who does.
  • Ask for your EA account details. Signing in to Ezzcoins (only needed for a watchlist) uses Google and never touches your EA account.
  • Use bots on EA's Transfer Market, or recommend tools that do. Both break EA's rules.
  • Let advertisers or anyone else pay for a card or a call to be featured.

Ads and independence

Ezzcoins is free and is paid for by advertising. Advertisers have no say over what the site says, and the trade desk's calls are made by fixed rules from price data alone. Ezzcoins is an independent fan project. It is not endorsed by or affiliated with EA or its licensors, and it is not affiliated with FUTBIN, FUT.GG, FUTWIZ or EasySBC.

Corrections and contact

Spotted a wrong price or card detail? Every player page has a link to report it, which sends the card's details with your note. For anything else, including questions and ideas, use the contact page. How the site handles your data is explained in Privacy and cookies.
