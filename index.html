      Core method

      The 84 floor

      In FC 27, Streamlined SBCs count Item Score, not squad rating. An 84 scores 830 while an 83 scores 410, yet both sit on the same ~650 coin floor. That makes 84s the cheapest score in the game.

      Buy
      Any 84 at the floor, name and league don't matter. Thursday morning is cheapest.
      Sell
      Into new Player SBCs (Friday and weekend releases) or submit them yourself.
      Watch
      If 85s drop below about 2.5× the 84 price, 85s become the better buy. The Fodder board flags it.
      Weekly

      Thursday dip, Friday flip

      Rivals rewards open millions of tradeable packs every . Champions starts Friday. The cheapest and dearest hours of the week sit about 36 hours apart.

      Buy
      –: 80–84 golds, sub-85 meta cards.
      Sell
      Thu evening to Fri before : meta cards, ICONs, campaign cards.
      Risk
      Don't panic-sell into the Thursday drop. It usually recovers within a day and a half.
      Specials

      Midweek trough on specials

      Heroes, ICONs and campaign cards drift down Monday to Wednesday while demand is thin, then climb into the Champions weekend.

      Buy
      Mon–Wed, cards that dropped 8–15% from their weekend price.
      Sell
      Friday before the new promo opens at .
      Risk
      Live cards (DFG, OTW) move with real-world results. Check fixtures first.
      Promo nights

      Sell before the drop, buy after

      New promo teams land every . Pack openings flood the market with older specials and high-rated golds for a few hours.

      Sell
      Friday afternoon, anything you won't use that weekend.
      Buy
      Friday late night to Saturday morning, once the first wave of pack pulls is listed.
      Skip
      Chasing new promo cards in their first hour. Prices start high and fall.
      Low budget

      Bid, don't buy

      Bid on auctions with under a minute left at 10–15% below the lowest Buy Now, then relist at Buy Now. The Flip calculator gives your maximum bid.

      Target
      Cards people actually use: meta golds, 84–86 fodder, popular chem styles.
      Margin
      You need at least ~5.3% just to cover tax. Aim for 8% or more.
      Trap

      Gallery hype

      EA confirms that completing a Gallery Set does not submit or remove your cards. SBCs do. So a price spike driven by Gallery collecting refills quickly, while SBC demand takes cards out of the market for good.

      Avoid
      Holding league or club cards after a Gallery-driven spike.
      Prefer
      Cards that SBCs eat. Grade a card in the Gallery first, then submit it.

      House rules

      1. Every sale loses 5% to EA. Break-even is your buy price ÷ 0.95.
      2. Check the live price on FUTBIN or FUT.GG before you buy. This page updates hourly; the market moves by the minute.
      3. A leak is not a fact. A new content drop only counts as reliable when both FUT Sheriff and FifaTradingRomania report it, or when it's live in the game.
      4. Never buy coins or use autobuyers or bots. EA bans accounts and wipes coins for both.
      5. Split big fodder buys across a few sessions. Clearing a whole page of floor listings pushes the price up.
      6. Keep some coins liquid on Wednesday night, so you can buy the Thursday dip.

      Where the data comes from

      Nothing else is used
      PricesFUT.GG and FUTBIN only. A call is always graded with the same site and page it was made from, because the two sites can disagree on a card's price.
      Rules, datesEA's own Pitch Notes and EA Help articles.
      Content dropsFUT Sheriff and FifaTradingRomania. Reported by both: reliable. Reported by one: don't trust it yet. Live in the game or announced by EA: confirmed.
      StrategyFifaTradingRomania and The FUT Accountant, when their latest posts or videos can be found. Their X, Threads and YouTube pages block automated reading, so the updater only sees what search results show. That can make a drop look single-sourced for a while even when both have posted it.
      PlayersThe player database uses EA's official FC 27 ratings, refreshed once a day. New cards and watchlist prices come from FUTBIN every hour.
      MistakesEvery tracked call is graded against real prices. Missed calls get a written lesson, and the updater reads those lessons before it makes new calls.
