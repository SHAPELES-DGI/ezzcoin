FC 27 trading guides

Six short guides to the setups Ezzcoins trades and grades: fodder value, the Thursday rewards dip, midweek buying, promo nights, small-budget bidding and market hype. Each one shows live data from the site.

01Fodder valueSBC fodder is where most coins quietly disappear. The cheapest card is not always the cheapest way to finish a challenge: what matters is how many coins you pay for each point a challenge asks for.02The rewards dipEvery Thursday morning (UK time) Rivals rewards pay out. Thousands of players open their packs at once and list what they don't need, so supply jumps and prices, fodder above all, often dip for a few hours.03Midweek specialsDemand for meta cards follows the week. Players build their squads for Champions weekend, so the cards they play are usually wanted most from Friday evening, and least early in the week.04Promo-night patienceNew promos usually arrive on Friday at 6 PM UK, and EA drops new content every evening at the same time. The first hour after a big drop is when prices are least reliable.05Small-budget biddingOn a small budget the 5% tax decides almost everything. A flip that looks like 300 coins of profit can be a loss once EA takes its cut, so start from the margin and only then bid.06Skip the hypeA card that jumps 20% in a day is exciting, and that is the problem: by the time it is trending, most of the move is usually over. Ask what is creating the demand before you buy.
