How the price read works

Ezzcoins records this card's price about once an hour and keeps hourly prices for 7 days and daily prices for 30 days. A single price far off the readings on both sides of it (a glitched or mispriced listing) is left out. The usual range leaves out the cheapest and dearest 10% of hours, so it shows where the card really traded. The time of day compares each price with the card's median over the 24 hours around it, so a card that rose all week doesn't look "cheapest at midnight", and it is only shown when the pattern held on most days. EA keeps 5% of every sale, so a card has to rise about 5.3% before a flip breaks even. Everything here describes the past: prices can move differently next week.
